## Primary studies that further restrict novelty

The targeted PubMed treatment-effect search found relevant work outside the legacy 100-paper collection:

- [*Precision Medicine Approach to Develop and Internally Validate Optimal Exercise and Weight-Loss Treatments…*](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32144896/): randomized IDEA trial data, 343 participants, individualized rules for diet/exercise options and WOMAC-related outcomes. This is a direct counterexample to any claim that individualized orthopedic treatment-rule learning is entirely new. Current evidence level in this package: verified abstract; original full text remains to be appraised.
- [*Exploring treatment effect heterogeneity of a PROMs alert intervention…*](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37392619/): causal forest analysis of an approximately 8,000-participant hip/knee arthroplasty trial. The intervention is postoperative monitoring, not the decision to perform arthroplasty. This distinction matters when defining the thesis question.
- [*Should BMI influence anterior versus posterior approach surgery in patients with CSM?*](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42066358/): a 1,085-patient multicenter analysis using a doubly robust framework to assess approach-specific outcomes. Current evidence level: abstract; eligibility and detailed assumptions need full-text review.

These examples establish prior work, not that these methods are unbiased or clinically ready. New searches and source appraisal may uncover closer competitors.
