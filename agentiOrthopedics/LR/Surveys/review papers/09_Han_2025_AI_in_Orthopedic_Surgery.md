## The same five shared new dimensions

These are the five additions proposed across **all ten reviews**, not a separate set for this paper. No explicit discussion of the defined dimensions was found in this review or in the other nine checked main articles.

| ID | Shared dimension | What to examine |
|---|---|---|
| N1 | Causal comparison of alternative treatment benefits | Estimate outcome differences under alternative treatments using explicit causal assumptions. |
| N2 | Empirical treatment support and a binding recommendation gate | Check treatment-group overlap and withhold unsupported comparisons. |
| N3 | Sensitivity of recommendations to unmeasured confounding | Assess whether unrecorded treatment-selection factors could overturn the recommendation. |
| N4 | Informative loss of follow-up outcomes | Assess bias when unobserved follow-up outcomes depend on recovery. |
| N5 | Treatment option sets that distinguish similarity from insufficient evidence | Separate clinically similar benefit from imprecise or unsupported comparisons. |

[Full definitions and cross-paper verification matrix](../review_dimensions/Five_Shared_New_Dimensions.md)

**Do not claim as new here:** reinforcement learning, multimodal LLMs, Bayesian uncertainty, federated learning, multiobjective optimization in general, or consent. The word “causality” appears in a legal-liability discussion; it is not a review of causal treatment-effect estimation.

**Classification note:** This is treated as a broad narrative review, rather than a protocol-driven systematic review.

## Meaning and verification limits

Covered dimensions include topics discussed as limitations or future directions; discussion does not establish clinical validation. The five shared new dimensions are the same N1-N5 for all ten reviews; no explicit discussion of their precise definitions was found in any checked main article, including tables and figure captions. Their novelty is relative to this ten-review set, and does not establish novelty across the research field. Separately hosted supplements and every cited primary study were not exhaustively audited.
