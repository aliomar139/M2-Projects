## The same five shared new dimensions

These are the five additions proposed across **all ten reviews**, not a separate set for this paper. No explicit discussion of the defined dimensions was found in this review or in the other nine checked main articles.

| ID | Shared dimension | What to examine |
|---|---|---|
| N1 | Causal comparison of alternative treatment benefits | Estimate outcome differences under alternative treatments using explicit causal assumptions. |
| N2 | Empirical treatment support and a binding recommendation gate | Check treatment-group overlap and withhold unsupported comparisons. |
| N3 | Sensitivity of recommendations to unmeasured confounding | Assess whether unrecorded treatment-selection factors could overturn the recommendation. |
| N4 | Informative loss of follow-up outcomes | Assess bias when unobserved follow-up outcomes depend on recovery. |
| N5 | Treatment option sets that distinguish similarity from insufficient evidence | Separate clinically similar benefit from imprecise or unsupported comparisons. |

**Do not claim as new here:** nonsurgical alternatives, psychosocial outcomes, costs, reduced questionnaire burden, or broad “value of information.” These already appear, sometimes as gaps in the reviewed tools. Table 2 uses “value of information” as a decision-aid evaluation measure.

Their novelty is relative to this ten-review set, and does not establish novelty across the research field. Separately hosted supplements and every cited primary study were not exhaustively audited.
