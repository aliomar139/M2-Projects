| ID | Shared dimension | What to examine |

| N2 | Empirical treatment support and a binding recommendation gate | Check treatment-group overlap and withhold unsupported comparisons. |
| N3 | Sensitivity of recommendations to unmeasured confounding | Assess whether unrecorded treatment-selection factors could overturn the recommendation. |
