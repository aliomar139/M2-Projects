# Ten review papers: 100 covered dimensions and five shared additions

Updated: 24 October 2026.

**The corrected requirement is five shared new dimensions across all ten reviews, not five different additions for each paper.** The 100 covered dimensions remain: ten per review.

A covered dimension includes a topic mentioned as a limitation or future direction; discussion does not imply implementation or clinical validation. A shared addition means no explicit discussion of its precise definition was found in any of the ten checked main articles, including embedded tables and figure captions. This does not establish novelty across the research field. Separately hosted supplements and every cited primary study were not exhaustively audited.

## Five shared additions

| ID | Dimension | Extraction question |
|---|---|---|
| N1 | Causal comparison of alternative treatment benefits | What is the estimated outcome difference under treatment A versus B, under stated causal assumptions? |
| N2 | Empirical treatment support and a binding recommendation gate | Does the system check overlap and withhold unsupported comparisons? |
| N3 | Sensitivity of recommendations to unmeasured confounding | Could plausible unrecorded treatment-selection factors overturn the recommendation? |
| N4 | Informative loss of follow-up outcomes | Could outcome-dependent follow-up loss bias the treatment comparison? |
| N5 | Treatment option sets that distinguish similarity from insufficient evidence | Does the system distinguish clinically similar benefits from imprecise or unsupported comparisons? |

[Definitions, examples, and the 10-review verification matrix](Five_Shared_New_Dimensions.md) | [Full-text audit](verification_audit.md)

The same five identifiers are used in every individual paper note. Repetition across files does not create additional dimensions.
