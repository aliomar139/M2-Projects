# Complete Paper Feature Extraction: Master 100-Paper Research Attribute Matrix

## 1. Overview and Analytical Purpose

This document provides the definitive research-attribute comparison matrix for all **100 papers** in the literature review on **Agentic AI and Machine Learning for Personalized Orthopedic Treatment Recommendation and Clinical Decision Support**.

In accordance with rigorous academic review methodology, **this table is organized strictly around common research features extracted from the literature**, rather than bibliographic or citation metadata. Each row represents a single research study, and the 10 analytical columns capture the structural data, cohort, clinical, methodological, and evaluative characteristics necessary to compare studies directly.

### The 10 Analytical Research Attributes
1. **Paper Title:** Full formal title of the research study.
2. **Dataset / Cohort Size:** Actual sample size (total patients, procedures, radiographs, train/validation/test splits, or NR if not reported).
3. **Dataset Description & Source:** Nature and origin of the data (national registry, multicenter prospective cohort, single-center EHR, public database like OAI, or in silico simulation).
4. **Patient / Population Characteristics:** Demographics, disease severity, surgical status, and inclusion criteria.
5. **Input Features / Variables:** Specific clinical variables, PROMs (OKS, WOMAC, KOOS, HAGOS), radiographic grades, 3D gait kinematics, wearable telemetry, or free-text narrative notes ingested by the AI model.
6. **Treatment / Intervention:** Medical procedures, surgical techniques (TKA, THA, hip arthroscopy, TSA), rehabilitation protocols, or conservative therapies under investigation (or *N/A* if non-interventional).
7. **Prediction / Recommendation Target:** Exactly what the AI system models (diagnostic classification, prognostic risk, patient outcome prediction, historical clinician decision prediction, counterfactual treatment-effect estimation, or individualized treatment recommendation).
8. **AI / ML Approach:** Explicit algorithm and architecture (XGBoost, Random Forest, CNN, DeepSurv, PPO DRL, Causal ML, LLM, RAG, Agentic AI).
9. **Evaluation Metrics:** Exact reported performance metrics (AUROC, C-index, MAE, Accuracy, Kappa, Calibration, DCA).
10. **Main Results / Limitations:** Summary of key quantitative findings, external generalizability, confounding risks, dataset shift, and translational limitations.

## 3. Standardization and Data Integrity Notes
- **NR (Not Reported):** Denotes attributes that the original authors could have measured/reported but omitted, preserving zero-hallucination compliance.
- **N/A (Not Applicable):** Denotes non-interventional studies (e.g., pure imaging diagnosis or general methodology) where clinical treatment interventions are not relevant.
- **Strict Separation of Decision Prediction vs. Recommendation:** Studies predicting what surgeons historically did (*Halvorson et al., 2026*) are strictly designated as *Historical Clinical Decision Prediction*, whereas studies estimating counterfactual treatment effects (*Curth et al., Nature Med 2024*) or dynamic reinforcement learning policies (*Dong et al., 2026*) are categorized as *Treatment Recommendation & Optimization*.
