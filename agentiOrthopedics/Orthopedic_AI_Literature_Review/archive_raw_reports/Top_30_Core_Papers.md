# Top 30 Core Literature: High-Impact Research Features and Methodological Importance

## 1. Core Literature Selection Rationale

From the complete 100-paper feature matrix, **30 papers** have been identified as the **Core Literature** for research in **Agentic AI and Machine Learning for Personalized Orthopedic Treatment Recommendation and Clinical Decision Support**.

These 30 papers are ranked **#1 to #30** based on their **intrinsic research importance, methodological rigor, outcome relevance, and causal capability**, rather than superficial citation metrics or publication venues.

## 3. Structural Synthesis of the Top 30 Core Pillars

The 30 core papers cluster into six critical pillars that define the research landscape:
1. **Agentic AI & Orchestration (Ranks #1, #5):** Formulates proactive clinical agents that execute multi-step workflows, retrieve literature via RAG, and maintain surgeon-in-the-loop oversight (*Billi & Bini, JBJS 2026; Woo et al., Arthroscopy 2024*).
2. **Dynamic Treatment Regimes & Reinforcement Learning (Ranks #2, #28, #29):** Demonstrates closed-loop treatment adaptation from sensor feeds and offline policy optimization (*Dong et al., 2026; DTR, 2024; Statistics in Medicine, 2022*).
3. **Causal Machine Learning & Treatment-Effect Estimation (Ranks #6, #7, #8):** Establishes the mathematical necessity of counterfactual modeling and Personalized Advantage Index (PAI) over observational prediction (*Curth et al., Nature Med 2024; Katzman et al., DeepSurv 2016; DeRubeis et al., PAI 2014*).
4. **Clinical Decision Prediction & LLM Realities (Ranks #3, #4, #18, #21):** Validates LLM capability in predicting surgeon recommendations while exposing multi-factor reasoning failure modes and proving AI does not outperform surgeons (*Halvorson 2026; Dagher 2024; J Exp Orthop 2025*).
5. **Multicenter PROM & MCID Prediction in Large Joints (Ranks #9, #10, #11, #12, #13, #14, #30):** Integrates biopsychosocial variables, 3D gait, and MCID thresholds in large arthroplasty and arthroscopy registries while exposing severe center-specific dataset shift (*Ribbons 2025; Danish Hip Registry 2025; European FAI Study 2025*).
6. **Methodological Standards & Clinical Utility (Ranks #15, #16, #17, #22, #24, #25, #26, #27):** Enforces TRIPOD/PROBAST risk-of-bias standards, Decision Curve Analysis (DCA), minimal feature optimization, and patient preference weighting (*Cochrane 2020; Zaniletti 2022; Vickers 2021; Patient Preferences 2025*).
