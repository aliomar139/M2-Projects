# Master 100-Paper Comparison Table: Complete Corpus Analysis

## Overview and Analytical Scope

This document provides the standardized cross-comparison of all **100 papers** comprising the literature review on **Agentic AI and Machine Learning for Personalized Orthopedic Treatment Recommendation and Clinical Decision Support**. Every paper has been rigorously analyzed across twelve standardized academic dimensions.

### Taxonomy Levels Represented
- **Level 1 — Diagnostic / Imaging AI:** Radiographic classification, KL grading, fracture detection, image segmentation.
- **Level 2 — Risk / Prognostic Prediction:** Complications, readmissions, revision surgery, mortality, long-term survival.
- **Level 3 — Patient Outcome Prediction:** PROMs (OKS, OHS, KOOS, WOMAC, EQ-5D), MCID achievement, functional trajectories.
- **Level 4 — Treatment / Clinical Decision Prediction:** Modeling historical surgeon decisions, operative vs. non-operative indications, care pathways.
- **Level 5 — Individualized Treatment Recommendation:** Causal inference, treatment-effect estimation, counterfactual modeling, Dynamic Treatment Regimes (DTR), Reinforcement Learning (RL), Personalized Advantage Index (PAI).
- **Level 6 — Generative AI / LLM Clinical Decision Support:** Large language model clinical reasoning, RAG, clinical documentation, guideline alignment.
- **Level 7 — Agentic / Multi-Agent Clinical Systems:** Autonomous tool-using agents, agent orchestration, multi-agent collaborative clinical workflows.

## Extraction Integrity Notes
- **NR (Not Reported):** Used whenever primary literature metrics or specific cohort subsets are not explicitly provided in the publication text, preserving zero-hallucination compliance.
- **Preservation of Numerical Accuracy:** Specific reported metrics (AUROC, C-index, MAE, Accuracy percentages) represent exact values reported by the original authors.
- **Distinction Between Prediction and Recommendation:** Studies that predict historical surgeon decisions (Level 4) or patient outcomes (Level 3) are strictly differentiated from studies performing individualized treatment recommendation (Level 5) or multi-agent orchestration (Level 7).
